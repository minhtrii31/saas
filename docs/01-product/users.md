# Users

## Primary User

People who want to improve their CV before applying for jobs.

Examples:
- Students
- Fresh graduates
- Junior developers
- Career switchers
- Job seekers preparing applications

## User Problems

Users often do not know:
- Whether their CV is clear and recruiter-friendly
- Whether their CV matches a job description
- Which skills or experiences are missing
- How to improve weak sections
- How to write a tailored cover letter
- How their CV improves over time

## User Goals

Users want to:
- Understand the quality of their CV
- Know what to fix first
- Compare their CV with a specific job
- Improve their chance of getting interviews
- Track their CV improvement history
